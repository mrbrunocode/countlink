Common questions

Is 45 minutes a common class-period length this suits?

Yes — it's one of the most common school class-period lengths, which is why it's offered as a quick-timer preset alongside custom durations.

Can students follow the countdown on their own phones too?

Yes — share the link and any device can open it; the projector and every phone will show the identical time remaining.

Does the timer keep going if the projector or Wi-Fi drops?

Yes, on any device that already has the page open — since the countdown is calculated locally against a timestamp in the URL, it doesn't need an ongoing connection to keep counting.
