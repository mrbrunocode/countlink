Common questions

Can I use this for a daily standup instead of a dedicated app?

Yes — set 10 minutes, drop the link in your team chat, and everyone's own screen counts down without installing anything or creating an account.

What happens when the 10 minutes run out?

Every open screen hits zero at the same instant and plays a short chime if sound is on. Nothing else happens automatically, so it's safe to leave running in the background.

Can I change 10 minutes to a different length later?

Yes — set a new duration and copy the new link. The mechanic is identical; only the timestamp inside the URL changes.
