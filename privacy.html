Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

The countdown tool itself collects nothing. The duration and label you set are encoded only in the URL in your own browser's address bar — they are never sent to, or stored on, any server operated by CountLink. There is no account system and no database.

QR codes. If you click "Show QR code," the current share link is sent to a third-party QR generation API (goqr.me) to render the image — this is the only feature on the site that makes an external request, it only ever happens if you explicitly click the button, and it only ever sends the share link itself (a duration and label, not anything personal).

Advertising. This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or generally at optout.aboutads.info.

Analytics. If enabled, this site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate traffic — which pages are visited and roughly how, not who you are individually. Analytics data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. You can block this with a browser extension such as Google's own Analytics opt-out add-on.

Cookies. Advertising and analytics providers may set cookies in your browser as described above. CountLink itself does not set any first-party cookies or use browser storage beyond the countdown state already visible in the page URL.

Children's privacy. This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. This policy may be updated as the site's advertising or analytics setup changes. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the update.

Contact. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@countlink.app.
