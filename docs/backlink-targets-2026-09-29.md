Sources: Bing Webmaster Tools → Backlinks → *Backlinks To Any Site* (compare
view, countlink vs stagetimer.io vs sharemytimer.live), plus live Bing SERPs for
"X alternatives" and "best timer for <use case>" roundups. Excludes anything
already in `outreach-ledger.json`.

ShareMyTimer is #1 on Bing with about as few links as we have, so on Bing the
wording on the page matters more than links (hence the 2026-09-29
"shareable/synced/group" copy change). Links are what Google needs: every
non-brand Google query still sits at position 40–90.

Stagetimer's 239 break down as roughly: conference/event sites linking their
timer (atlanticcouncil 110, tedx, urucon, atoms2024, daga2026…), founder
podcasts/interviews (~20 podcast hosts, starterstory), startup directories,
AV/streaming ecosystem partners, winget package mirrors (desktop app, n/a),
and a few university resource pages. CountLink's embed snippet already
carries the "Shared countdown by CountLink" attribution link, which is our
equivalent of the event-site links. It pays off as embeds get used.
